# 4. The person who teaches learns first (in chat)

Use this before explaining a goal you do not know well. Attach the source for that goal.
Then, make a note in the kit: `python3 -m estudo tutor --objetivo <id> --minutos <quanto tempo>`.

---

I’m going to teach [goal, e.g., cellular respiration] to [name], who learns [learning style, e.g., by seeing and with a real-life example].
Use only the attached source.

1. Explain it to me as if I were a beginner, in short steps, in the order things happen.
2. Give me a real-life example for each step.
3. Tell me the 3 most likely test traps, each with the passage from the source that shows the correct answer.
4. Ask me 3 questions to check if I understood, one at a time, and correct me using the passage from the source.
5. If the source doesn’t say something, say “the source doesn’t say” instead of filling in the gaps.
