# 1. Plan before building (in chat)

Paste this into Claude chat (or another AI). Attach whatever you have: photos of notes, the teacher’s guide,
the graded test, pages from the book. Replace what’s inside [brackets].

---

I want to put together a study guide for [who: my daughter / my son / my student], who is in [subject and level].
Don’t build anything yet: first, let’s plan it together.

How the teacher teaches and assesses: [e.g., divides the subject into objectives; if you get a question wrong, you lose the entire objective;
lessons with text-heavy slides and little practice].
How [name] learns: [e.g., very visual, draws well, needs real-life examples and the chance to ask questions].
What [name] got wrong on the last test: [list or attach the test].
The materials I have: [notes, teacher’s guide, digital book].

1. Analyze what I sent and tell me what you understood in a few lines.
2. Ask me everything you don’t know that would help you make a better guide for both of us.
3. Give me 2 or 3 options for how to organize the guide, with the pros and cons of each, so I can choose.
4. Fixed rule: all content and every question in the guide must come only from the material I provide. If anything is missing, ask me; don’t fill in the gaps from memory.
